THE CHALLENGE ```

Problems in Agriculture

Agriculture is affected by several challenges that can influence crop health, productivity, and farmers' income.

🌦️

Changing Weather

Changes in rainfall, temperature, and humidity can affect crop growth and farming decisions.

🍂

Plant Diseases

Plant diseases can damage crops. Identifying possible diseases early can help farmers seek suitable advice.

🌱

Crop Selection

Choosing crops without considering soil and climate conditions can lead to unsuitable farming decisions.

SMART TECHNOLOGY ```

How AI Helps Agriculture

Artificial Intelligence can analyse data and images to support agricultural decisions. Machine Learning models can learn patterns from datasets and use them to make predictions on new inputs.

🌾

Crop Recommendation

A trained Machine Learning model can analyse information such as nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, temperature, humidity, soil pH, and rainfall to recommend a suitable crop from its supported categories.

🔬

Plant Disease Detection

An image-classification model can analyse an uploaded plant leaf image and predict a possible disease or healthy-leaf category supported by its training data.
